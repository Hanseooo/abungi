# Abungi Art Bible

## North star

**Handcrafted cardboard battle theatre.** Every playable character, enemy, elite, boss, and environment should look like an illustrated paper/cardboard cutout assembled on a tabletop stage. It is playful and tactile without becoming a cluttered scrapbook.

## Shared illustration rules

- Three-quarter or readable front/side pose suitable for a cutout standee.
- Strong silhouette at ~100–180 CSS px tall on mobile.
- Transparent or easily removable background for identity art.
- Slightly exaggerated proportions and props; avoid photoreal anatomy.
- Limited expressive shading, rough ink outline, printed/cardboard texture, minor edge wear.
- Similar line weight, camera angle, rendering density, shadow vocabulary, and edge treatment across the full set.
- No embedded text, logos, UI, watermarks, emoji, or avatar-circle framing.
- No glossy sci-fi HUD aesthetic, glassmorphism, neon AI gradients, or unrelated art-pack mixing.
- Subtle vernacular/sign-painting influence is welcome; overt national imagery is not the direction.

- **Michael** — disciplined soldier silhouette, rifle held safely across body, clean utility shapes.

- **Hans** — inventor with tool rig, small sentry/drone components, leaner tech silhouette.
- **Yeeho** — gambler with loaded dice/cards/tokens, quick off-balance pose suggesting calculated risk.
- **Jiro** — chef with pan and serving kit, grounded supportive silhouette.
- **Nathaniel** — dark/mystic entity with torn layered paper wisps and restrained eerie negative space.

### Normal enemies

Scrapper, Road Dog, Cutpurse, Smokehead, Hexling, Wisp, Dronelet, Bulwark Bot, Backstreet Medic, and Tin Brute must each be recognizable from silhouette/prop alone. Their affinity accent may support recognition but may not be the only differentiator.

### Elites

- **The Broker** — sharp paper tags/contracts/coin pressure; sly Trick silhouette.
- **Ironclad** — layered plated cardboard, broad defensive mass, Tech hardware.
- **Night Maw** — torn layered jaw/void shape with Mystic sustain cues.

### Bosses

- **Jonlow, the Glutton** — expanding stacked-food/plate/cardboard silhouette; sustain/escalation readable without comedy overwhelming threat.
- **Klyde, the Psycho** — unstable angular pose, mismatched attack props, bounded visual chaos.
- **The Warden** — imposing Tech controller with modular drone interface; phase escalation should be visible through pose/VFX, not enemy-intent UI.

## Environments

Three region scenes share the tabletop theatre perspective but vary in background geometry and material palette. The title stage is the theatrical opening curtain/table. Backgrounds should stay quiet enough that HP/status/UI remains legible.

## VFX and animation

Use cutout transforms, opacity, shadow displacement, paper particles, simple SVG/CSS projectiles, heal bursts, smoke, and impact shake. Do not require sprite sheets. Animation should reinforce event semantics: lunge → hit-stop → damage pop; heal → upward bob/burst; summon → assembly/arrival; KO → tilt/fall/fade.

## Acceptance checklist

An asset is accepted only when it: matches the established set, reads at mobile size, has a distinct silhouette, contains no text/watermark/UI, works against all three region scenes, and does not introduce a conflicting rendering style.
